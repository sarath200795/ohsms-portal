Shared across every module

Multi-org & RBAC

Admin, Safety, Engineering, Operations, Technician — with per-user permission overrides and an approval queue for new members.

Append-only audit

Every change is written to an immutable trail. Rules block update and delete, so the record cannot be rewritten after the fact.

Scan-to-act

QR tokens on extinguishers, isolation points and permits open a public read-only view; signing in unlocks the actions your role allows.

Sessions & idle timeout

Session persistence plus an idle timer with a countdown warning, so a shared shop-floor terminal signs itself out.
